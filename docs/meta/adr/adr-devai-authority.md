# ADR: DEVAI Authority over the Legacy SGP Protocol

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-07-11

## Decision

The owner declared the pinned DEVAI Constitution authoritative over SGP's
legacy constitution and agent protocol. DEVAI substrate, role, evidence, and
documentation-governance rules prevail when they conflict with legacy SGP
rules. SGP product semantics continue to extend the DEVAI reference signal when
they do not conflict.

## Consequences

DEVAI framework and meta documentation roots are current. Doctor, schema,
inventory, sensor, and scorecard contracts are enforced as adoption gates.
Authority conflicts are resolved in favor of DEVAI unless superseded by a later
explicit human owner decision.
